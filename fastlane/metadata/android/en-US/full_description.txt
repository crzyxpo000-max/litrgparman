A blue window cracked open in the sky. The System arrived and gave humanity 365 days: level up enough to be accepted as a full Layer, or get harvested.

Everyone got the standard System. You got the broken one.

Ascension Protocol is a single-player, fully text-based LitRPG for Android. It reads like a novel, but every page is your call, and some of those calls come knocking 30 days later.

◆ RISE AS AN ANOMALY
The glitched voice in your head, Echo, shows you what nobody else can see: enemies' real levels, hidden quest rewards, the lines the System doesn't want read. You're the only one with a Daily Quest. Hide your power, or put it on display for everyone, but every reveal gets you noticed by something higher up.

◆ A CLASS BUILT FROM HOW YOU PLAY
You don't pick a class off a menu. The System watches how you fight and what you choose, then makes you an offer. Eight base classes, hidden classes, branching skill evolutions, skill fusion, titles, professions and Grades from F to S.

◆ THE REAL CHOICES ARE MORAL
You'll get stronger no matter what. The real question is what kind of power you become: protector, tyrant, god or liberator. Mercy, Order and Humanity axes; delayed consequences that come back weeks later; a Story Director that keeps the world turning without you.

◆ PEOPLE WHO REMEMBER YOU
Characters with their own goals, fears and memories. Trust, respect, fear and loyalty. Companions, rivals, betrayals. Death is permanent.

◆ FOUR ACTS, NINE ENDINGS
The way back from the Tutorial Layer, a wrecked harbor town, the 100-floor Spire, three alien worlds and the final 35 days. Factions, a great betrayal, an invasion and the core of the System itself. Eight endings, plus a hidden ninth. The ending screen shows you what the world became because of you.

◆ FEATURES
• Full English and Turkish support; switch languages in-game, instantly
• Fully offline: no ads, no in-app purchases, no internet permission
• Tactical combat with stances, finishers and the environment; weak enemies get crushed in a single line
• Auto-save, 3 save slots and a permadeath Ironman Mode
• System, Light, Dark and AMOLED themes
